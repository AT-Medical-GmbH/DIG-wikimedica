# Code of Conduct

Wikimedica is a medical knowledge project. We work respectfully, factually and with patient
safety in mind.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and assume good faith; discuss content, not people.
- Back claims with sources; accept correction, especially from qualified reviewers.
- Respect confidentiality: no patient data, no personal data of colleagues, ever.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and AI assistance.
- Use inclusive language; sex- and gender-sensitive medicine is a core topic of this project.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, promotion of products or
unproven therapies, and knowingly publishing medically unsafe content.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to conduct@wikimedica.de (placeholder mailbox — to be confirmed by the owner).
Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or block participation. Patient-safety
concerns about published content follow the safety-hold process in
[`docs/editorial/content-lifecycle.md`](docs/editorial/content-lifecycle.md).
