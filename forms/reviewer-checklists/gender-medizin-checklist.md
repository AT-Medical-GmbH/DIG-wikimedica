# Sex/Gender Cross-Check Checklist (Gender-Medizin) — Wikimedica

**Version:** 1.0
**Use for:** **every** professional article, therapy protocol, pharmaka article and guideline summary
before approval. Patient-facing types use Section 7 as a short check.
**Reviewer:** Gender-Medizin Editor, or the Specialty Editor for articles outside Gender-Medizin.

Terms: **sex** = biological characteristics (male, female, intersex); **gender** = social and identity
aspects (women, men, trans, non-binary). Use them precisely and do not interchange them.
